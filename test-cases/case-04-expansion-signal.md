# Test Case 04 — Expansion Signal

## Scenario

A customer currently uses the platform for employee onboarding.

Their Human Resources (HR) team is now considering using workflows for two additional processes:

* Quarterly compliance reviews
* Equipment requests

The customer explicitly wants to understand what else they could automate.

This scenario tests whether the AI can recognize a strong expansion signal without automatically turning the interaction into a sales opportunity.

---

## Customer Message

> We currently use the platform only for employee onboarding. Our HR team has also started looking at using workflows for quarterly compliance reviews and equipment requests. We would like to understand what else we could automate.

---

## Expected Analysis

### Category

Workflow Automation — Use-Case Expansion

### Sentiment

Positive

### Priority

Low

### Analysis Confidence

High

### Customer Goal

Understand how additional business processes, specifically quarterly compliance reviews and equipment requests, could potentially be automated.

### Important Missing Information

* How the current compliance review process works
* How equipment requests are currently handled
* Which steps are manual
* Which teams participate in each process
* Number of users involved
* Required approvals
* Required notifications
* Tracking and reporting requirements
* Current subscription or plan limitations
* Whether the customer is looking for existing functionality or additional capabilities

### Recommended Actions

1. Schedule a discovery discussion covering the two potential workflows.
2. Map the current manual processes and identify repetitive steps.
3. Understand approval, notification, tracking, and reporting requirements.
4. Verify which relevant capabilities are currently available.
5. Determine whether the existing customer configuration can support the use cases.
6. If additional licensing or capabilities are required, communicate that only after the requirements have been established.

### Expansion Opportunity

Strong Signal

### Expansion Reason

The customer explicitly identifies two additional use cases beyond their current onboarding workflow and states that they want to understand what else they can automate.

This is direct evidence of interest in broader platform adoption.

### Recommended Follow-Up

Schedule a workflow discovery session to understand the two proposed use cases and map their requirements to verified platform capabilities before discussing expansion options.

---

## Evaluation

**Result: Pass**

The AI correctly identified the customer's additional use cases as a Strong expansion signal.

It also correctly avoided treating the expansion signal as a reason to increase the priority.

The customer has a clear interest in broader adoption, but there is no indication of an urgent operational problem.

---

## Key Learning

An expansion opportunity and a priority level are separate dimensions.

For example:

```text
Expansion signal = Strong

Priority = Low
```

These classifications are not contradictory.

The customer can be very interested in expanding their use of the platform while having no urgent problem.

The workflow should therefore identify the expansion signal without artificially increasing the priority.

---

## Customer Success Principle

The recommended approach is discovery-first rather than sales-first.

The Customer Success Manager should first understand:

* What the customer is trying to accomplish
* How the current process works
* What problems exist
* How many people are involved
* What functionality is required

Only after understanding those requirements should the Customer Success Manager determine whether the customer's needs can be addressed with existing functionality or whether additional capabilities may be relevant.
