# Test Case 01 — Workflow Automation

## Scenario

A customer is preparing to onboard approximately 50 new employees next month.

Their managers currently have to manually check whether each employee has completed their onboarding tasks. This takes several hours every week.

The customer wants to know whether this process can be automated.

---

## Customer Message

> We are onboarding approximately 50 new employees next month. Right now our managers have to manually check whether every employee completed their onboarding tasks. This is taking several hours every week. Is there a way we can automate this process?

---

## Expected Analysis

### Category

Workflow Automation — Onboarding Process

### Sentiment

Neutral

### Priority

Medium

### Analysis Confidence

High

### Customer Goal

Reduce or eliminate the manual effort required to verify employee onboarding completion.

### Important Missing Information

* Current onboarding workflow
* Tasks that need to be monitored
* How completion is currently tracked
* Whether managers need notifications or reports
* Existing integrations involved in the process
* Desired automation behavior

### Recommended Actions

1. Understand the current onboarding workflow.
2. Identify which steps are currently manual.
3. Determine what the customer wants to happen automatically.
4. Verify whether the required functionality is supported.
5. Identify possible configuration or workflow options.

### Expansion Opportunity

Potential

### Expansion Reason

The customer is preparing for a larger onboarding activity and is looking to automate an existing process. This could potentially lead to broader workflow adoption.

### Recommended Follow-Up

Conduct a workflow discovery discussion before the next onboarding cycle to understand the current process and desired automation.

---

## Evaluation

**Result: Pass**

The AI correctly identified the workflow automation use case, recognized the recurring manual effort, and classified the priority as Medium.

The analysis also identified relevant missing information without assuming specific product capabilities.
